JellyMold Companion

A permanent record of everything each person watches and listens to on this server. Each person can read their own history from an app; admins can read everyone's. History is never deleted by the plugin.

Checking…

What to record

Trailers, theme music, extras and Live TV are never recorded.

Who to record

Everyone is recorded unless unticked here. Unticking someone stops new recording; what is already recorded stays.

Counting

Shorter plays are still kept, with their seconds; they just don't count as watching.

Helping Jellyfin

When an app is closed mid-play, Jellyfin 10.10 stops it some minutes later at a position it guessed, minutes past where the viewer was, and can tick a nearly finished episode as watched. This puts the resume point back where they really were, and takes back a watched tick only the guess earned. It changes Jellyfin's own watch data, so it is off until you choose it.
Each night, extracts the embedded subtitles of newly added films and episodes, so they appear at once the first time they are chosen. Jellyfin keeps what it extracts, picture-based subtitles included, which can take tens of megabytes a film.

Keeping

Samples are the raw position reports, kept for diagnosing problems. The history itself is always kept.

Past history

Everything watched before the plugin was installed comes from imports. Each person's watched items in Jellyfin are imported automatically as their starting point. Other sources are imported here: each shows what it would add before anything is written, and each can be undone.
